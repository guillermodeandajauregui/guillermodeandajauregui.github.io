## Open. Free. Collaborative.


I am a biomedical researcher working at the intersection of complex systems, network science, and computational biomedicine.

Together with students and collaborators, we investigate biological and biomedical questions spanning cancer, neurodegeneration, systems pharmacology, infectious diseases and microbiomics, and computational public health.

Our research brings together computational methods, biological data, and interdisciplinary perspectives to understand complex systems and address meaningful biomedical problems.



## Nohoch data. Chan lab. 



Science advances through shared ideas, transparent methods and independent verification. We develop and reuse open tools, publish computational workflows, and look for collaborations that connect methods to meaningful biomedical questions.

## What we work on

Our current research spans [AI for cancer](/research/cancer/), [neurodegeneration and multiomics](/research/neuro/), [systems pharmacology](/research/pharmacology/), [microbiomics and infectious diseases](/research/microbiomics/), [network epidemiology, and computational public health](/research/public-health/). Network science and complex-systems methods connect these areas.

Our computational work is driven by open questions, interpretable methods and research partnerships that cross disciplinary boundaries.
