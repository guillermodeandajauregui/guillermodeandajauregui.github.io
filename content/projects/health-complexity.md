We build reproducible geographic data pipelines integrating compatible levels of observation across national sources, including health facilities, census variables and economic units.

## Current focus

- Harmonized municipal and AGEB geographic identifiers.
- Efficient columnar storage with explicit source metadata.
- Network and accessibility measures connecting populations to care resources.
