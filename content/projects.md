# Projects

Our projects bring together computational approaches, biological questions, and collaborators across disciplines. Here are three ongoing research directions; detailed project cards and outputs will be added as the work develops.

## Featured projects

- **Multimodal AI for Breast Cancer**: Integrating imaging, transcriptomic and clinical data; supported by Alianza Médica para la Salud (AMSA).
- **Alzheimer’s Disease Network Biology**: Molecular network organization and differences between early- and late-onset Alzheimer’s disease.
- **Systems Pharmacology & Pharmacovigilance**: Drug perturbations, adverse drug reactions, polypharmacy and network-based strategies.
