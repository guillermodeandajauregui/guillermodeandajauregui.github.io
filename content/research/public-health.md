We use complex systems, spatial information and epidemiological records to study population-level patterns and healthcare-system organization.

## Current directions

- Geographic integration of open Mexican health-system data.
- Hospital demand and geographic healthcare accessibility.
- Spatial inequalities and health determinants.
- Network-based approaches to epidemic spread and healthcare utilization.
